How it works

Claude Cowork simplifies knowledge work through a straightforward four-step process that puts AI autonomy to work for you.

01

Grant Folder Access

Open Claude Desktop and navigate to the Cowork interface. Select the specific folder where you want Claude Cowork to operate. The agent can only access files within folders you explicitly grant—ensuring complete control over your data. This folder becomes the workspace where Claude Cowork reads existing files and creates new ones.

02

Describe Your Task

Tell Claude Cowork what you need in natural language. No technical expertise required—simply describe the outcome you want. Examples: "Create an expense report from these receipt screenshots," "Organize my downloads folder by file type," or "Draft a summary report from my meeting notes." Claude Cowork understands context and intent.

03

Watch Autonomous Execution

Claude Cowork analyzes your request, creates an execution plan, and begins working autonomously. The interface displays real-time progress updates as the agent reads files, processes information, and creates outputs. Sub-agents handle parallel tasks when beneficial, optimizing for speed while maintaining quality. You stay informed without needing to micromanage.

04

Review and Refine

When complete, review the results directly in your workspace folder. Claude Cowork creates new files, updates existing ones, or reorganizes structures as requested. Need adjustments? Provide feedback in natural language and Claude Cowork will refine the output. The agent learns from your preferences to improve future task execution.

FAQ

Do I need to code?

No. Describe the outcome in plain language. Cowork plans the steps, executes inside a secure VM, and drops finished files into your local folders.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Cowork only works on folders you authorize and performs actions inside an isolated VM. You can review each step and keep full control over access.

What types of output can it deliver?

Professional, native files your team already uses, including Excel spreadsheets with formulas, PowerPoint decks, and Word documents.

Can it handle long-running tasks?

Yes. Cowork is designed for longer workflows without session timeouts, so it can finish multi-step tasks from start to delivery.

Who can join the Research Preview?

It is available to Claude Max subscribers on macOS using Claude Desktop.

How does the live mission control work?

A dedicated control surface shows how Cowork plans, executes, and delivers work. You can see every step before it runs, monitor actions in real-time, pause, or intervene at any time. Claude breaks goals into parallel sub-tasks, executes them inside an isolated VM with your approval, and delivers finished files to your local folders.

What is sub-agent collaboration?

Complex goals are automatically split into parallel sub-tasks that run simultaneously for faster outcomes. Instead of handling everything sequentially, Cowork coordinates multiple agents working on different parts of the same project.

How secure is the VM isolation?

Every action runs inside a dedicated virtual machine, protecting your system and ensuring clear boundaries between AI automation and your core environment. You have real-time oversight, can grant only the folders needed for each task, and maintain full control with an audit trail of every action.

What are some practical use cases?

Cowork handles multi-step office work like financial analysis (aggregating CSVs into polished reports), market research (collecting sources and formatting briefs), and document cleanup (renaming, categorizing, and summarizing thousands of files). It's designed for real office work that requires coordination across multiple files and formats.

Can I see the plan before it executes?

Yes. Claude creates a human-readable, step-by-step approach and confirms with you before execution. You always know what will happen before automation runs, giving you full visibility and control.

What system requirements do I need?

You need macOS with Claude Desktop installed and an active Claude Max subscription. The work happens locally in an isolated VM, so your files never leave your machine.
