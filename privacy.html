Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Cowork conceptual landing page handles data. The site is not affiliated with Anthropic and does not collect personal information.

Last updated: 2026-01-13 Applies to: landing page and guide

Scope

This policy covers the Cowork landing page, guide, and legal pages hosted in this project. It does not cover any official Anthropic or Claude services.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information and we do not provide user accounts, uploads, or forms. If anonymous analytics are enabled for this static site, they would be limited to:

  • Aggregate page views and time-on-page metrics.
  • Basic device or browser metadata (such as browser type).
  • Approximate location derived from IP address, in aggregate only.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, or precise location data. The landing page does not access your local files or run any tasks on your device.

How information is used

Anonymous metrics are used only to understand which sections are most useful and to improve the clarity of the content. We do not sell data and we do not use information for targeted advertising.

Third-party links

The site links to external resources. When you leave this site, the privacy policy of the destination site applies.

Data retention

Any anonymous analytics, if enabled, are retained only as long as needed for aggregated reporting and are not tied to individual visitors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to the project maintainer.
